EyeEase Attention Tracker — Chrome Extension

1. Extract the ZIP to a normal folder (do not load Chrome from inside the ZIP).
2. In PowerShell, open that folder and run:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\setup-assets.ps1
   Or on macOS/Linux: bash setup-assets.sh
3. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked,
   and choose this extracted folder (the folder containing manifest.json).
4. Click EyeEase > Open eye tracker > Start camera > Calibrate.

Note: MediaPipe model and WASM dependencies are downloaded by the setup script.
The heuristic is experimental and cannot determine actual attention with certainty.
